The Cirya Ethics Charter

01. Neural Neutrality

We provide infrastructure that is inherently neutral. Data is delivered without mediation or biased routing protocols.

02. Zero-Retention Policy

Cirya does not harvest, store, or sell user metadata. Our business model relies on infrastructure lease, not information extraction.

03. Decentralized Identity

Staff and partner authentication is handled through decentralized protocols, ensuring that access remains secure and private.
